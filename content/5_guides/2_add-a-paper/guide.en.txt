Title: How to add a paper

----

Intro: Share a research paper by pasting its DOI. Title, authors and journal are filled in for you, so it only takes a minute or two.

----

Audience: Authors and editors who share research

----

Duration: 2 minutes

----

Cta: Add a paper

----

Steps: 
-
  title: "Open “Add a paper”"
  text: "Sign in, then click **Add a paper** in the header. No account yet? Step 1 of *How to write a story* explains how to create one."
