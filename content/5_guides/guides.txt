Title: Guides

----

Intro: Short, illustrated how-tos for writing on Inkwell. Every step shows exactly where to click.

----

Faq:

-
  question: Who can write for Inkwell?
  answer: Anyone. Create a free account with “Become a contributor” at the bottom of any page.
-
  question: Why doesn’t my story appear on the site straight away?
  answer: An editor reads every story and paper before it goes live. You can keep editing it until then, and you’ll find it on your desk in the meantime.
-
  question: Can I change a story after it’s published?
  answer: Ask an editor. Published stories are locked for contributors so that what readers see has always been reviewed.
-
  question: What’s a DOI, and where do I find it?
  answer: A DOI is a permanent ID for a research paper. It usually starts with “10.” and is shown on the paper’s page, often as a link like https://doi.org/10.1038/…
-
  question: Why wasn’t the abstract filled in for my paper?
  answer: Some publishers don’t allow their abstracts to be copied. In that case, describe the paper in a few sentences of your own. Your description is credited to you.
