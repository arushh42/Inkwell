Title: Do Plain-Language Summaries Increase the Reach of Research? A Field Study

----

Abstract: We randomly assigned 214 papers to receive a plain-language summary on an institutional blog. Over six months, papers with summaries received 2.3 times more full-text views and were more frequently shared outside academia, with no change in citation counts. We provide guidelines for writing summaries that are accurate, accessible and quick to produce.
