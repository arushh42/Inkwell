Title: Lightweight Content Models for Scholarly Web Publishing

----

Abstract: Scholarly groups increasingly publish research summaries, blogs and preprints on their own websites, yet most content management systems are designed for marketing sites. We describe a lightweight content model with three entity types — stories, papers and authors — and evaluate it with twelve research groups. Editors completed publishing tasks 38% faster than with their previous systems, and pages met accessibility guidelines without manual intervention. We discuss implications for flat-file and serverless deployments.
