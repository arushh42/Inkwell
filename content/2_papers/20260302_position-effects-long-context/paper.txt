Title: Position Effects in Long-Context Retrieval Revisited

----

Abstract: We revisit the observation that language models under-use information placed in the middle of long inputs. Across eight open models and three tasks we find that the effect is strongly modulated by distractor similarity rather than input length alone. A simple quote-then-answer prompting strategy recovers 71% of the mid-context accuracy gap. We release our evaluation suite to support reproducible comparison.
